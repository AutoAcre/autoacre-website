Common questions

The plain answers, on the record.

We're a small operation in the Northern Rivers building something specific. If you've got a question that isn't covered here, the site assessment is the right place to ask it.

RTK is centimetre-accurate satellite positioning. The mower knows where it is to within two centimetres — same paths every cycle, no buried boundary wire, no missed strips. LiDAR is the laser-based obstacle-avoidance system: fallen branches, garden hoses, sleeping dogs, kids' bikes — the system stops or routes around. Together they're the dividing line between a robotic toy for a quarter-acre lawn and an autonomous system that actually handles acreage.
Two parts. You BUY the autonomous mowing system outright — reference launch price $33,490. RTK base, mower, professional install, boundary mapping. From there, AutoAcre handles ongoing operation under a tiered monthly MANAGEMENT fee — $165 at 2.5 acres up to $650 at ten. Maintenance, blades, firmware, monitoring. Twice-weekly summer mowing, weekly winter — about three times a fortnightly contractor. The system is yours; the service is ours.
Engineered for 2.5 to 10 acre acreage — premium lifestyle properties, rural residential estates, larger landowners. Below 2.5 acres, a consumer wire-bounded mower is usually a better fit. Beyond ten acres, scoped as commercial deployments and quoted individually.
